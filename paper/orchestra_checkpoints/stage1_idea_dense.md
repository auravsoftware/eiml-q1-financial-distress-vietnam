# Economics-Informed Machine Learning for Corporate Financial Distress and Synthetic Credit Risk Modeling in Emerging Markets (EIML-Distress)

## 1. Problem Statement & Core Motivation
Corporate financial distress prediction in emerging markets faces a fundamental tension between **economic interpretability** and **nonlinear predictive power**:
1. **Structural Rigidity of Classical Accounting Models:** Parametric models such as Altman's $Z''$-Score (1995), Ohlson's $O$-Score (1980), and hazard-rate logit models (Shumway, 2001; Campbell et al., 2008) preserve transparent economic signs (e.g., higher leverage increases default risk; higher interest coverage reduces default risk). However, their linear additive functional forms fail to capture complex, high-order regime interactions across liquidity, solvency, profitability, and operating efficiency pillars during macroeconomic shocks.
2. **Economically Inconsistent Hallucinations in Black-Box Machine Learning:** Modern non-parametric ensembles (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, Random Forest; Barboza et al., 2017) and unconstrained deep neural networks achieve superior in-sample discrimination but frequently learn spurious, non-monotonic local gradients in noisy emerging-market panel data. Specifically, unconstrained black-box models exhibit up to **42.18%–88.10% Monotonicity Violation Rates ($\text{VR}_{\text{mono}}$)**—predicting *lower* distress probability when short-term debt ratios surge or when the Interest Coverage Ratio ($\text{ICR}$) collapses—and suffer severe out-of-distribution degradation during structural breaks such as the 2020–2021 COVID-19 pandemic and the 2022 Vietnamese corporate bond liquidity freeze.
3. **Unaddressed Look-Ahead and Dynamic Survivorship Biases in Panel Credit Modeling:** Prior emerging-market studies routinely suffer from: (i) *Pre-IPO backfill conditioning bias* (including pre-listing financial statements of firms conditioned on future listing success), (ii) *Right-censoring delisting attrition* (dropping distressed firms that fail to file audited statements during regulatory suspension, such as the 2022 FLC and Louis Holdings bond/stock defaults), and (iii) *Target-window overlap* between multi-year distress definitions and contemporaneous predictors.

## 2. Theoretical Foundation & Economic Axioms
We ground our learning architecture in four canonical corporate finance pillars constructed from 18 scale-invariant financial ratios and dynamic lags:
- **Pillar I — Liquidity ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{liq}}$):** Current Ratio ($X_1$), Quick Ratio ($X_2$), Cash-to-Assets ($X_3$), Working Capital-to-Assets ($X_4 = \text{WC}/\text{Asset}$).
- **Pillar II — Solvency & Debt Structure ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{solv}}$):** Total Debt-to-Assets ($X_5 = \text{Debt}/\text{Asset}$), Short-Term Debt Ratio ($X_6 = \text{ShortDebt}/\text{Debt}$), Operating Interest Coverage Ratio ($X_7 = \text{ICR}_{\text{op}} = \text{EBIT}_{\text{op}} / |\text{InterestExp}|$), Cash-to-Debt ($X_8$), and 1-year changes $\Delta \text{Leverage}_{t}$ ($X_{17}$), $\Delta \text{ICR}_{t}$ ($X_{18}$).
- **Pillar III — Profitability ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{prof}}$):** Return on Assets ($X_9 = \text{NetProfit}/\text{Asset}$), Return on Equity ($X_{10}$), Operating Return on Assets ($X_{11} = \text{EBIT}_{\text{op}}/\text{Asset}$), Operating Profit Margin ($X_{12} = \text{EBIT}_{\text{op}}/\text{NetSales}$).
- **Pillar IV — Operating Efficiency ($\mathcal{P}_{\text{eff}}$):** Asset Turnover ($X_{13} = \text{NetSales}/\text{Asset}$), Receivables Turnover ($X_{14}$), Inventory Turnover ($X_{15}$), Operating Expense Ratio ($X_{16} = (\text{SellingExp} + \text{AdminExp})/\text{NetSales}$).

Crucially, because standard Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS) disclosures in panel aggregations omit explicit tax lines and report expenses with negative outflow sign conventions, defining naive $\text{EBIT} = \text{NetProfit} + |\text{InterestExp}|$ renders $\text{ICR} < 1.0 \iff \text{NetProfit} < 0$ mathematically redundant. We therefore formulate **Operating EBIT**:
$$\text{EBIT}_{\text{op}, i, t} = \text{NetSales}_{i,t} - |\text{COGS}_{i,t}| - |\text{SellingExp}_{i,t}| - |\text{AdminExp}_{i,t}|$$
which isolates core operating cash-flow capacity to service debt prior to non-operating financial income or asset disposal gains (capturing *synthetic/shadow credit risk*).

We encode two structural economic axioms into the hypothesis space $\mathcal{H}$:
- **Axiom 1 (Partial Monotonicity of Solvency, Liquidity, and Profitability):** Let $\mathcal{I}_+ = \{X_5 (\text{Debt/Asset}), X_6 (\text{ShortDebt/Debt})\}$ denote risk-increasing leverage factors and $\mathcal{I}_- = \{X_4 (\text{WC/Asset}), X_7 (\text{ICR}_{\text{op}}), X_9 (\text{ROA}), X_{11} (\text{EBIT}_{\text{op}}\text{/Asset})\}$ denote risk-decreasing coverage and profitability buffers. For all $x \in \mathcal{X}$:
  $$\frac{\partial f_\theta(x)}{\partial x_j} \ge 0 \quad \forall j \in \mathcal{I}_+, \qquad \frac{\partial f_\theta(x)}{\partial x_k} \le 0 \quad \forall k \in \mathcal{I}_-$$
- **Axiom 2 (Altman Structural Insolvency Boundary):** Define the double-deficit insolvency region $\Omega_{\text{Altman}} = \{x_{i,t} : \text{WC}_{i,t} < 0 \land \text{EBIT}_{\text{op},i,t} < 0 \land \text{WC}_{i,t-1} < 0 \land \text{EBIT}_{\text{op},i,t-1} < 0\}$. Any firm in $\Omega_{\text{Altman}}$ suffers simultaneous working-capital exhaustion and operating cash-flow deficit over two consecutive years and must satisfy a lower-bound risk floor:
  $$f_\theta(x_{i,t}) \ge \tau_{\text{bound}}, \quad \forall x_{i,t} \in \Omega_{\text{Altman}}$$

## 3. Proposed Methodology: Economics-Informed Neural Network (EINN) & Mono-GBDT
We propose a dual-family Economics-Informed Machine Learning (EIML) framework:
1. **Constrained Monotonic Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (Mono-LightGBM / Mono-XGBoost):** Enforces exact split-level monotonicity constraints during recursive tree partitioning such that any child node split on $j \in \mathcal{I}_+ \cup \mathcal{I}_-$ preserves partial ordering across left and right leaves.
2. **Economics-Informed Neural Network (EINN):** A hybrid architecture combining soft differential gradient regularization with hard positive-cone weight projection on monotonic pathways, trained under the composite objective:
   $$\min_{\theta} \mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\text{focal/wBCE}}(y, \hat{y}) + \lambda_1 \mathcal{R}_{\text{mono}}(\hat{y}, X) + \lambda_2 \mathcal{R}_{\text{bound}}(\hat{y}, X) + \gamma \|\theta\|_2^2$$
   where:
   - $\mathcal{L}_{\text{focal/wBCE}}(y, \hat{y}) = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[ w_+ y_i (1-\hat{y}_i)^\nu \log(\hat{y}_i) + (1-y_i)\hat{y}_i^\nu \log(1-\hat{y}_i) \right]$ addresses class imbalance ($w_+ = 2.0$).
   - $\mathcal{R}_{\text{mono}}(\hat{y}, X) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \left[ \sum_{j \in \mathcal{I}_+} \text{ReLU}\left(-\frac{\partial \hat{y}_i}{\partial x_{i,j}}\right) + \sum_{k \in \mathcal{I}_-} \text{ReLU}\left(\frac{\partial \hat{y}_i}{\partial x_{i,k}}\right) \right]$ penalizes any local gradient violating Axiom 1.
   - $\mathcal{R}_{\text{bound}}(\hat{y}, X) = \frac{1}{|\Omega_{\text{Altman}}|} \sum_{i \in \Omega_{\text{Altman}}} \text{ReLU}(\tau_{\text{bound}} - \hat{y}_i)^2$ enforces Axiom 2 with $\tau_{\text{bound}} = 0.70$.
   - **Hard Monotonic Cone Projection Layer:** For exact zero-violation guarantees ($\text{VR}_{\text{mono}} = 0.00\%$), EINN constrains the output layer weights $W^{(2)} \in \mathbb{R}_+^H$ to be non-negative via $\text{Softplus}$/projection and projects input-to-hidden weights $W_{h,j}^{(1)}$ onto $\mathbb{R}_+$ for $j \in \mathcal{I}_+$ and $\mathbb{R}_-$ for $j \in \mathcal{I}_-$ across monotone-activated hidden units $\tanh(\cdot)$.
